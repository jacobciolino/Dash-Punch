# Micro USB

Micro USB uses the Seeker casting clip, a code-native pixel-art American Type A
two-prong plug projection, and square-pixel electricity on the latched victim.
The plug follows existing punch aim, extension and charge; its ivory housing,
flat blades and cyan sparks use chunky rectangular pixels. These effects do not
affect collision, reach or timing.

The opening punch retains normal parry and Dash Escape rules. A landed punch
attaches the existing three electrical pulses, 0.16 seconds apart. These pulses
cannot be parried or Dash Escaped and do not open post-hit escape windows.
The opening hit's existing post-hit escape window is preserved; after the first
electrical pulse, combo-breaker commands are unavailable for that latch.
Existing invulnerability and combo-ownership cancellation still end the latch.
Damage, hitstun, resource recharge and all other characters remain unchanged.
